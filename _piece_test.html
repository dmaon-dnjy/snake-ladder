    📜 Rules — Cards & Shooters Edition

    Pieces: 1–4 per player (set at setup, default 4), Lv1→Titan (1–4). Land on a cell already holding your own piece(s) and you get a choice: level up (merge — levels sum, cap 4) or keep them separate.

    Turn: Roll 1–6, choose a piece. Exact 100 to win. 6 = extra turn.

    🃏 Card cells 11·22·33·44·55·66·77·88·99: On landing you choose: take 1 🪙 or draw a card. The card stays face-down until you draw, then flips to reveal it — once drawn, you take it into your hand (no switching to a coin). You play cards from hand on your turn before rolling. 💀 Death Trap 13: any piece that lands on 13 is destroyed and sent back home. Deck: lots of ⬜ Empty (discard), 🪙 coins (+1/+2/+3 & penalties -1/-2), ➡️ moves (+2/+4/+6, -2/-4/-6, ⬆️ row up +1, ⬇️ row down -1 — pick a piece to move), and specials. Shuffles when empty.

    ⚔️ Combat: When a piece lands on a cell held by an opponent's piece, the opponent's piece is immediately destroyed and removed from the board — just like classic Snakes & Ladders. No coin toss, no duel. The attacker keeps their piece on the cell.

    Specials — only ONE each: ♜ Rook Shooter, ♝ Bishop Shooter, ♛ Queen Shooter, 🔄 Swap. All cards are kept in hand.

    🔫 Shooters: Before rolling, spend a gun charge to shoot an opponent piece back to Start. Rook = same row/col (like rook in chess), Bishop = diagonal (like elephant/bishop), Queen = either (like queen). Shooter piece must be on board; target must be on board and in line. Cost 1 charge.

    🔄 Swap: Play from hand before rolling — pick one of YOUR pieces, then an opponent piece; they trade squares.

    🪙 Coins: Start with 3. 🪙 Money cells (+1 when any piece lands there) give bonus coins.
    🌀 Hurricane: when piece lands there, its NEXT move goes backward -roll, once only.
    🐊 Swamp: when piece lands there, that SPECIFIC piece skips its next turn (other pieces still playable).
    🛗 Elevator: when a piece lands on it, it rides exactly one row up (⬆️) or down (⬇️) to the cell directly above/below in the same column — never to a fatal or special cell. Special cells are random each game (8 money, 5 hurricane, 5 swamp, 8 elevator) and never overlap cards/1/100/💀13.

    ⏳ Cooldown: After moving a piece, that piece skips the player's next turn only, then becomes available again. Each piece has an independent cooldown counter. Anti-deadlock ensures you always have at least one playable piece.

    🧊 Freeze: The Wizard ability freezes an opponent piece for 2 full turns. While frozen, the piece cannot move, be chosen, or participate in merges. The freeze expires automatically.

    🛒 Shop & Abilities: During your turn, you may either Roll & Move or Use an Ability — you cannot do both in the same turn.

    Shop items (one of each globally):

    • ♜ Rook Gun — 5 🪙. Shoot horizontally/vertically (like a rook). Destroy a target piece. Single-use.
    • ♝ Bishop Gun — 10 🪙. Shoot diagonally (like a bishop). Destroy a target piece. Single-use.
    • ♛ Queen Gun — 18 🪙. Shoot horizontally, vertically, or diagonally (like a queen). Destroy a target piece. Single-use.
    • 🧙 Wizard — 25 🪙. Freeze an opponent piece for 2 turns. Single-use.

    Open the Shop (🛒 button) during your turn, spend coins, and the ability appears in your Abilities panel. Click it to enter targeting mode — valid targets are highlighted. Click a valid target to execute, or cancel. Using an ability ends your turn immediately.
